Product 01 · ReclaimNet · Live

Confidence-scored liveness for IP address space.

Your IPAM says an address is in use. The wire says nothing's there. ReclaimNet reconciles the two — gathering evidence from independent collectors over time, scoring how alive each address really is, and writing the verdict back into NetBox.

It never declares an IP dead on a hunch. It watches, accumulates proof, and only commits when it's confident — and a human always approves the write.

  • Confidence over time — days of multi-collector evidence, not a single snapshot.
  • Two-way NetBox reconciliation — documented-but-dead, and alive-but-undocumented.
  • Modular collectors — ARP, active probe, NetFlow next; add a source, the core never changes.
  • Human-in-the-loop writes — review every change before it touches your source of truth.
